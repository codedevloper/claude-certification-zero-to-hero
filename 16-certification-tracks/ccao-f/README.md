## What the credential is for

The Claude Certified Associate – Foundations (exam code CCAO-F) certifies that you can use Claude to get real
business work done with little hand-holding. In practice that means five things:

1. Getting structured business tasks done with Claude without step-by-step guidance.
2. Using the built-in features of the Claude apps (Projects, Artifacts, research, connectors, memory, Skills) to
   streamline a workflow.
3. Spotting where a process could be improved with Claude.
4. Picking an approach that balances quality, speed and cost.
5. Knowing Claude's limits, and handing complex or technical work to people with Claude Architect or Developer skills.

It is the entry-level credential in the Claude Certification Program. It is about **judgement while using the Claude
apps**. It does not test coding, the API or agent architecture.

## Who it is for

In the official guide's terms, the target candidate relies on Claude day to day to get work done and **builds Claude
Projects**, typically in an operations, marketing, project-management, teaching, communications or other knowledge-work role. That includes
internal staff who run AI-assisted workflows day to day and external consultants who help clients find use cases and
redesign processes. The Partner Academy page also pitches it to consultants, sellers and delivery leads in partner
organisations.

It is **not** aimed at software developers building against the API, ML specialists or people designing enterprise AI
architecture. Those topics belong to the Architect and Developer credentials. If you are an engineer, this track is
still a good warm-up for [CCAR-P](../ccar-p/README.md), because the judgement it teaches (verify outputs, protect data,
choose the right tool) carries over.

### The minimally qualified candidate

Exam items are pitched at a *minimally qualified candidate*: someone who just meets the bar. Picture a person who:

- uses Claude regularly at work, not just for one-off questions;
- structures prompts deliberately and breaks big jobs into steps;
- is comfortable with Projects, Artifacts and similar features, and has moved past simple question-and-answer use
  towards redesigning processes;
- understands where AI creates value in their organisation, where adoption risks sit, and how responsible-use
  practices apply;
- knows the practical limits of AI: hallucinations, context constraints and data sensitivity.

If that description fits you on most days, you are close. If several parts sound unfamiliar, plan for the longer study
sequence below.

## Format and logistics

All figures come from [`registry.yaml`](../registry.yaml) (verified 2026-10-24).

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Items | 60, multiple-choice and multiple-response (each item says how many answers to pick) |
| Time | 120 minutes (Pearson suggests planning for about 135 minutes of seat time, including check-in) |
| Delivery | Pearson VUE: online proctored (OnVUE) or at a Pearson test centre |
| Passing standard | Scaled score of 720 on a 100–1,000 scale, criterion-referenced (you are measured against a fixed standard, not other candidates) |

The per-domain percentages on your score report help you see weak areas, but your pass or fail result depends only
on the total scaled score.

## Registration and eligibility

- **Who can sit it.** Certification is open to people at organisations in the **Claude Partner Network**. You register
  with a partner email address on a recognised company domain; personal email addresses are not accepted. Candidates
  must be at least 18. Customers and non-partners cannot certify at the time of writing (as of 2026-10-24). The free
  [Anthropic Academy](https://anthropic.skilljar.com/) courses are open to everyone but award completion
  certificates, not certifications.
- **Partner tier.** The Associate credential does not count toward Claude Partner Network tier eligibility; the
  Developer and Architect credentials do.
- **How to register.** Go to the CCAO-F certification page on the Anthropic Partner Academy, download the exam guide,
  read the Certification Terms and Conditions and the Exam Policy, register and pay, then create your Pearson VUE
  account and schedule either online proctoring or a test centre.
- **Name match.** The name on your Pearson profile must match your government-issued photo ID exactly. Corrections go
  to Anthropic certification support before you schedule (see [exam-day.md](exam-day.md)).

## Retakes and renewal

- **Retakes.** Wait 14 days after a first failed attempt, 30 days after a second and 90 days after a third. You may
  attempt the same exam at most four times in a rolling 12 months. Each attempt costs the full fee.
- **Renewal.** The credential lasts 12 months. Renewing before expiry costs nothing: you catch up on what
  has changed, then pass an unproctored check on the Anthropic Partner Academy. Let it expire and you sit (and pay for)
  the whole exam again.
  Anthropic may require a full retake if exam content changes significantly.

## Recommended study sequence

The order below follows dependencies, not domain numbers.

| Phase | Focus | Suggested time |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundations | [What LLMs are](../../01-ai-and-llm-fundamentals/03-large-language-models.md), [hallucinations](../../01-ai-and-llm-fundamentals/06-hallucinations.md), [context windows](../../01-ai-and-llm-fundamentals/05-context-windows.md), [how Claude works](../../02-claude-fundamentals/01-how-claude-works.md) | 2–3 days |
| 2. Prompting (D1) | Domain 1 study guide plus exercises 1.1–1.3 | 2–3 days |
| 3. Products and models (D3, D5) | Domains 3 and 5 together: build a real Project with instructions and knowledge | 3–4 days |
| 4. Evaluation (D2) | Domain 2, the heaviest domain. Practise fact-checking on your own outputs every day | 3–4 days |
| 5. Responsible use (D6) | Domain 6 plus redaction exercise | 2 days |
| 6. Workflows (D4, D7) | Domains 4 and 7: redesign one real workflow end to end and troubleshoot it | 3 days |
| 7. Consolidation | Mock exams, misconceptions, revision notes, readiness check | 3–5 days |

That comes to roughly three to four weeks at one to two hours a day. If you already use Claude daily at work, the
[15-day plan](../../00-getting-started/roadmaps/15-day-preparation.md) or even the
[7-day accelerated plan](../../00-getting-started/roadmaps/7-day-accelerated.md) may be enough. Newcomers to AI should
follow the [30-day plan](../../00-getting-started/roadmaps/30-day-structured.md).
