# Acme Logistics Travel and Expense Policy

Version 3.2, effective 2026-07-01. Owner: Finance Operations. This is a fictional policy written for training purposes.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to all Acme Logistics employees and contractors who travel on company business.

1.2 It does not cover relocation, which is handled under the separate Relocation Policy.

## 2. Booking travel

2.1 All flights, rail and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal unless the portal cannot offer the route.

2.2 Flights must be booked at least 14 days before departure where possible. Bookings made fewer than 7 days before departure need written approval from a director.

2.3 Economy class is the standard for all flights. Premium economy is allowed for a single flight segment longer than 6 hours.

2.4 Business class is not reimbursed unless the Chief Financial Officer approves it in writing before booking.

## 3. Accommodation

3.1 The nightly hotel limit is 180 EUR in capital cities and 130 EUR elsewhere, excluding local taxes.

3.2 Employees may stay with friends or family instead of a hotel and claim a flat allowance of 40 EUR per night.

## 4. Meals and daily allowances

4.1 Meals are reimbursed at actual cost up to a daily maximum of 65 EUR per person.

4.2 Alcohol is not reimbursed, except as part of an approved client entertainment event under section 6.

4.3 Receipts are required for every meal claim, whatever the amount.

## 5. Ground transport

5.1 Public transport is the default for travel between airports, stations and hotels.

5.2 Taxis and ride-hailing services may be used when public transport is unavailable, unsafe, or would add more than 45 minutes to the journey.

5.3 Mileage for a personal car used on company business is reimbursed at 0.30 EUR per kilometre.

## 6. Client entertainment

6.1 Client entertainment must be approved in advance by the employee's line manager.

6.2 The limit is 90 EUR per attendee, including the employee, and the names and organisations of all attendees must be listed on the claim.

## 7. Submitting claims

7.1 Expense claims must be submitted through the expenses system within 30 days of the end of the trip.

7.2 Claims submitted after 90 days are not paid unless Finance Operations grants an exception.

7.3 Lost receipts must be replaced by a signed missing-receipt declaration. No more than two declarations are accepted per employee in a calendar year.

## 8. Non-compliance

8.1 Claims that break this policy may be rejected in full or in part.

8.2 Repeated or deliberate breaches are handled under the Disciplinary Procedure.
