# Northwind Lending support handbook (fictional, synthetic)

This handbook is invented for a training lab. Northwind Lending is not a real company and none of these policies describe a real lender.

## 1. Opening hours and channels

- The support desk is staffed from 08:00 to 20:00 Monday to Thursday, 08:00 to 18:00 on Friday, and 09:00 to 13:00 on Saturday. It is closed on Sunday.
- The hardship hotline keeps the same hours as the support desk but is also open from 10:00 to 14:00 on public holidays.
- Secure messages sent outside opening hours are answered within one business day. Agents must not promise a faster turnaround.
- Live chat is available only to customers who are signed in to the Northwind app.

## 2. Identity checks

- Before discussing an account, agents confirm the customer's full name, date of birth and the last four characters of the loan reference.
- If two checks fail, the agent ends the call politely and records "identity not confirmed" in the case notes. The agent does not say which check failed.
- Agents never ask for a full card number, a full bank account number or a password. If a customer offers one, the agent stops them and explains that Northwind never needs it.

## 3. Address and contact changes

- Mailing address changes use form NL-ADR-2 in the case tool. Email and phone changes use form NL-CON-1.
- A change of address triggers a confirmation letter to both the old and the new address.
- Changes requested by a third party (for example a relative) need a signed authority form on file first.

## 4. Payment arrangements

- A payment holiday pauses repayments for up to two months. Interest continues to accrue and the term is extended by the same number of months.
- A payment plan reduces the monthly amount for up to six months. The shortfall is spread over the remaining term.
- Agents may offer a payment holiday once in any twelve-month period. Any second request goes to the hardship team.
- Autopay dates move to the next business day when the scheduled date falls on a weekend or public holiday. Customers are notified by email two days before.

## 5. Complaints and escalation

- A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction where the customer expects a response. Agents log it in the complaints queue the same day.
- Tier 1 agents resolve what they can within three business days. Unresolved complaints move to Tier 2 automatically.
- The escalation matrix is stored in the Operations space of the intranet under "Escalation matrix v7". Agents should not keep local copies.
- Complaints about fees, vulnerable customers or data handling go straight to Tier 2.

## 6. Records and retention

- Call recordings are kept for 18 months, then deleted automatically.
- Uploaded documents are kept for the life of the loan plus six years.
- Case notes must be factual, free of opinions about the customer, and must not contain full account numbers.

## 7. Document uploads

- Customers upload payslips, bank statements and identity documents through the app or the upload portal.
- An upload confirmation email is sent automatically. Agents should tell customers that review usually takes two business days.
- Unreadable or expired documents are rejected with a reason code from list NL-DOC-R.

## 8. Tone and language

- Use plain English, short sentences and the customer's preferred name.
- Do not use jargon such as "arrears capitalisation" without explaining it.
- Avoid promises about outcomes that depend on another team's review.
