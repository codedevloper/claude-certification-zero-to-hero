# Returns and Damaged Goods Policy

Acme Logistics handles returns on behalf of its retail customers. This document explains how warehouse staff and customer-care agents process a return request from start to finish.

## Return window

A shipper may request a return within 30 calendar days of the proof-of-delivery timestamp. Requests made after day 30 need written approval from the account manager. Perishable goods (category P) have a shorter window of 72 hours because the stock cannot be resold after that point.

## Damaged on arrival

When a consignee reports damage, the agent must collect at least two photographs: one of the outer carton and one of the damaged item. The claim is opened in the Claims Desk system with reason code DMG-01. Claims under 250 euros are approved automatically. Claims of 250 euros or more go to the claims supervisor, who must decide within two business days.

## Refused deliveries

If a consignee refuses a parcel at the door, the driver scans it with status RTS (return to sender). The parcel travels back to the origin depot on the next linehaul. The shipper is charged the standard return fee of 6.50 euros per parcel unless the refusal was caused by an Acme error, such as a wrong address label printed in our depot.

## Restocking

Returned items that pass inspection are restocked within 48 hours. Items that fail inspection are moved to quarantine bay Q3 and the shipper decides within 14 days whether to dispose of them, repair them or have them shipped back.
