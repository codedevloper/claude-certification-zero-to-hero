# Driver Safety and Hours Rules

These rules protect Acme Logistics drivers and other road users. They apply to employed drivers and to subcontracted carriers working under an Acme contract.

## Driving hours

A driver may drive for at most 9 hours in a day. Twice a week this can be extended to 10 hours. After 4.5 hours of driving, the driver must take a break of at least 45 minutes, which may be split into 15 minutes followed by 30 minutes.

## Fatigue reporting

Any driver who feels too tired to drive safely must stop at the next safe place and call the night desk. Nobody is disciplined for a fatigue report. The night desk arranges a relief driver or reschedules the delivery.

## Vehicle checks

Before the first trip of the day the driver completes a walk-around check covering tyres, lights, mirrors, brakes and load security. Defects are logged in the fleet app. A vehicle with a safety-critical defect, such as a brake warning light, must not leave the yard until a mechanic clears it.

## Incidents

After any collision, however minor, the driver photographs the scene, exchanges details and reports the incident to the fleet manager within one hour. The fleet manager opens an incident record with code INC-ROAD.
