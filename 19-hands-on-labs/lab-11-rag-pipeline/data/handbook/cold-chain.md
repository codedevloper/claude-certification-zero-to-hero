# Cold-Chain Handling Standard

This standard applies to every temperature-controlled consignment that Acme Logistics moves, including pharmaceuticals, fresh food and laboratory samples.

## Temperature bands

Acme operates three bands. Frozen goods are kept at minus 18 degrees Celsius or colder. Chilled goods are kept between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius. Controlled-ambient goods are kept between 15 and 25 degrees Celsius. The band is printed on the shipping label as F, C or A.

## Data loggers

Every chilled or frozen pallet carries a data logger that records the temperature every five minutes. Drivers download the logger at delivery and attach the file to the proof of delivery. A logger that is missing or unreadable must be reported to the cold-chain coordinator before the end of the shift.

## Excursions

A temperature excursion is any reading outside the band for more than 30 consecutive minutes. When an excursion happens, the consignment is placed on hold and must not be delivered. The cold-chain coordinator reviews the logger trace and decides, together with the shipper's quality team, whether the goods can be released. Pharmaceutical consignments with an excursion are never released without written sign-off from the shipper's qualified person.

## Vehicle pre-cooling

Reefer trailers must be pre-cooled for at least 45 minutes before loading. The driver records the trailer temperature at the start of loading on the handheld device.
