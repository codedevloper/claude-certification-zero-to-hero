# Warehouse Security Handbook

This handbook describes physical and information security practices in Acme Logistics warehouses.

## Visitor access

Visitors sign in at reception, show photo identification and wear a red visitor badge at all times. A visitor must be escorted by an Acme employee in every area beyond reception. Visitor badges are collected at sign-out and logs are kept for 90 days.

## High-value cage

Consignments declared above 5,000 euros are stored in the high-value cage. Access to the cage requires two people, each using their own badge, and every opening is recorded on camera. Camera footage for the cage is retained for 31 days.

## Handheld devices

Handheld scanners lock after 5 minutes without use and require the user's PIN to unlock. A lost or stolen handheld must be reported to the IT service desk immediately so the device can be wiped remotely.

## Reporting a security concern

Staff report suspicious activity, tailgating or lost badges to the site security lead. Reports can also be made anonymously through the ethics line. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.
