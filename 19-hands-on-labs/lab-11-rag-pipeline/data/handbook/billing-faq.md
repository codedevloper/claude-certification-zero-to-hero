# Billing FAQ for Shippers

This FAQ answers common billing questions from shippers who use Acme Logistics.

## Invoice schedule

Invoices are issued on the first business day of each month and cover all consignments delivered in the previous month. Payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date.

## Fuel surcharge

The fuel surcharge is reviewed every month. It is calculated from the national average diesel price published in the previous month and is shown as a separate line on the invoice.

## Disputes

A shipper can dispute an invoice line within 60 days of the invoice date by opening a ticket with category BILL-DISPUTE. The disputed amount is put on hold and does not accrue late-payment interest while the dispute is open. The billing team aims to resolve disputes within 10 business days.

## Late payment

Invoices unpaid 15 days after the due date incur interest at 1 percent per month. After 45 days overdue, new bookings for that shipper are paused until the balance is cleared.
