# Northwind Lending Customer Support Handbook

Fictional reference material for training. Version 2026.10.

## 1. Who we help

Northwind Lending offers personal loans between 1,000 EUR and 25,000 EUR with terms from 12 to 60 months. Support agents help applicants and existing borrowers with applications, documents, repayments and account changes. Agents do not make credit decisions; the underwriting team does.

## 2. Tone and conduct

Be calm, specific and brief. Use the customer's name once at the start of a reply. Never speculate about whether an application will be approved. Never ask for a full card number, a password or a one-time passcode in chat. If a customer shares one, tell them to change it and do not repeat it back.

## 3. Application stages

An application moves through five stages: submitted, documents pending, under review, decision issued, and funds released. Customers can see the stage in the online portal. Typical times are: documents pending until the customer uploads everything; under review for two to three working days; funds released within one working day of signing the agreement.

## 4. Required documents

Every applicant must provide proof of identity, proof of address dated within the last three months, and proof of income. Proof of income is the two most recent payslips for employees, or the latest tax return for self-employed applicants. Bank statements are requested only when the underwriter asks for them. Documents must be uploaded through the portal; agents cannot accept documents by email for security reasons.

## 5. Common upload problems

If an upload fails, check that the file is a PDF, JPG or PNG under 10 MB. Photos of documents must show all four corners and be readable. Password-protected PDFs are rejected automatically; the customer should save an unprotected copy. If the portal shows "upload received" but the stage does not change within one hour, raise a ticket to the documents team with the application ID.

## 6. Repayments

Repayments are collected by direct debit on the date the customer chose at signing. A customer may change the collection date once every twelve months through the portal. Overpayments are allowed at any time without a fee. Early settlement is allowed; the settlement figure in the portal is valid for 28 days.

## 7. Payment difficulties

If a customer says they may miss a payment, thank them for telling us early and transfer them to the Customer Care team, who can discuss options. Agents must not promise payment holidays, reduced payments or fee waivers themselves. Note the conversation on the account with the tag CARE-REFERRAL.

## 8. Complaints

Treat any expression of dissatisfaction as a potential complaint. Acknowledge it, apologise for the experience without admitting liability, and log it in the complaints tool within one working day. Tell the customer they will receive a written acknowledgement within five working days and a final response within eight weeks.

## 9. Account changes

Customers can update their email address and phone number in the portal. Changes to a legal name or postal address need supporting documents uploaded through the portal. Agents cannot change bank details for direct debits; the customer must do this in the portal, which triggers a security check.

## 10. Escalation contacts

Documents team: for stuck uploads and document queries. Underwriting liaison: for questions about what a document must show, never for decision outcomes. Customer Care: for payment difficulties and vulnerable customers. Complaints team: for anything logged as a complaint. Security team: for suspected fraud, account takeover, or a customer who has shared a password.

## 11. Closing a conversation

Before closing, summarise what was agreed, state any next step and who owns it, and give the expected time frame. If a ticket was raised, give the ticket number. Ask whether there is anything else, and close politely.
