# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This is a learning community. People arrive with very different backgrounds, from someone writing their first prompt
to architects running AI platforms at scale. We commit to making participation respectful, welcoming and free of
harassment for everyone, whatever their experience, identity or background.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind to beginners. There are no stupid questions here.
- Critique ideas, not people. Explain *why* something is wrong and point to a source.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Credit others' work, and respect intellectual property and licences.
- Respect exam integrity: never share, request or hint at real exam content.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing other people's private information
- Sharing or soliciting exam dumps, recalled questions or answer keys
- Posting malicious code, live credentials, or instructions intended to harm real systems
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when you represent the
project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method listed on the repository's GitHub profile, or
with GitHub's "Report content" feature. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently ban participants whose
behaviour breaks this code. Responses are proportionate: a correction for a first, minor issue, and removal for severe
or repeated harm.
