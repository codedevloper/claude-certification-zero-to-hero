# Memo: change of statement printing vendor

Synthetic document for training purposes. All organisations and figures are fictional.

To: Customer Operations Leadership, Northwind Lending
From: Procurement
Date: 3 October 2026

Northwind Lending will move customer statement printing from Harbourline Print to Clearwell Mail Services on 1 January 2027. The current contract with Harbourline ends on 31 December 2026 and will not be renewed.

The tender received four bids. Clearwell scored highest on the weighted evaluation with 82 points out of 100, ahead of the incumbent with 74 points. Clearwell's quoted unit price is 0.41 dollars per statement, compared with 0.47 dollars today. At the current volume of 1.9 million statements a year, the expected saving is about 114,000 dollars a year.

Clearwell subcontracts large print runs to a second facility. The third-party risk assessment rated the vendor medium risk for this reason. The signed contract requires Clearwell to give 60 days' notice before changing any subcontractor and allows Northwind to audit both facilities once a year.

Two test mailings will run in November using synthetic customer data only. Production data will not be sent to Clearwell until the second test passes the address-quality check, which requires at least 99.5% of addresses to be accepted by the postal validation service.

Customer Operations should expect a short rise in contacts after the first statements are sent from the new vendor, because the envelope design and return address will change. Procurement recommends a short notice on the customer portal in December explaining the change.

Decision requested: Customer Operations Leadership is asked to approve the portal notice by 14 November.
