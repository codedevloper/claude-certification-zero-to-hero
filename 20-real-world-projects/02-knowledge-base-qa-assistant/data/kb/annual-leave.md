# Annual leave policy

Owner: People Team, Acme Logistics. Version 6.3, reviewed 5 January 2026. Synthetic policy for training use.

## Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 27 days of annual leave per year plus public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on contracted hours. After five years of service, the entitlement increases by two days.

## Booking leave

Book leave in the HR system at least two weeks in advance for periods longer than three days. Your line manager approves or declines within five business days. Warehouse and transport roles follow the seasonal leave calendar, which limits leave during the November to January peak.

## Carry-over

You may carry over up to five unused days into the next leave year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are lost, unless they could not be taken because of sickness or parental leave.

## Sickness during leave

If you are ill during annual leave and provide a medical certificate, the affected days are restored to your balance.
