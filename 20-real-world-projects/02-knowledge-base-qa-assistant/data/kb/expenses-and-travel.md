# Expenses and business travel policy

Owner: Finance Operations, Acme Logistics. Version 4.2, reviewed 1 June 2026. Synthetic policy for training use.

## Submitting expense claims

Submit expense claims in the expenses portal within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Claims older than 90 days are rejected unless the finance controller approves an exception in writing. Attach an itemised receipt for every item over 25 euros. Card statements are not accepted as receipts.

Your line manager approves claims up to 2,000 euros. Claims above 2,000 euros also need approval from the budget holder for your cost centre. Approved claims are paid in the next monthly payroll run.

## Hotels

Book hotels through the travel desk so that bookings appear in the duty-of-care tracker. The nightly limit including taxes is 180 euros in most cities. The higher limit of 260 euros applies in London, Paris, Zurich and New York. Stays above the limit need pre-approval from your director.

## Meals and per diem

For domestic travel the meal per diem is 45 euros per day. For international travel the per diem follows the country table published on the finance intranet page. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Client entertainment must be pre-approved and recorded in the gifts and hospitality register.

## Flights and rail

Economy class is standard for flights under six hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of six hours or longer. Rail is preferred over flights for journeys under four hours door to door.
