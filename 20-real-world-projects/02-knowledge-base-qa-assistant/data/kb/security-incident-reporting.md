# Security incident reporting procedure

Owner: Security Operations Centre, Acme Logistics. Version 5.0, reviewed 2 September 2026. Synthetic procedure for training use.

## What to report

Report anything that might affect the confidentiality, integrity or availability of company information. Examples include phishing emails, lost devices, unexpected password reset messages, data sent to the wrong recipient and unknown people in restricted areas.

## How to report

Use the "Report phishing" button in the mail client for suspicious emails. For everything else use the security incident form on the intranet or call the Security Operations Centre, which is staffed 24 hours a day. Do not try to investigate the incident yourself and do not delete evidence.

## Response targets

The Security Operations Centre acknowledges reports within 15 minutes. High-severity incidents are escalated to the on-call incident manager immediately. Where personal data may be affected, the data protection officer is informed within 24 hours so that the 72-hour regulatory notification deadline can be met.

## After an incident

Every high-severity incident has a blameless post-incident review within ten business days. Actions from the review are tracked in the risk register until they are closed.
