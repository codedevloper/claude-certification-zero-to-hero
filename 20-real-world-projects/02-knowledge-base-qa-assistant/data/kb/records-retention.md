# Records retention schedule (summary)

Owner: Legal and Compliance, Acme Logistics. Version 1.4, reviewed 18 May 2026. Synthetic schedule for training use.

## Retention periods

Customer contracts are kept for seven years after the contract ends. Shipment and customs records are kept for ten years. Employee personnel files are kept for six years after employment ends. CCTV recordings from depots and offices are kept for 31 days and then overwritten automatically, unless they are preserved for an investigation.

## Legal hold

When Legal and Compliance issues a legal hold, normal deletion stops for the records named in the notice until the hold is released in writing.

## Disposal

Paper records are disposed of in locked confidential waste bins. Electronic records are deleted by the system owner at the end of the retention period, and the deletion is recorded in the retention log.
